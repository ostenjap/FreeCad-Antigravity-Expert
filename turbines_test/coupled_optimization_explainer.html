Swarm Orchestration

Multi-Physics Turbine Blade Swarm Optimization

Understanding the automated feedback loop that optimizes external aerodynamics, conjugate heat transfer, and structural stress in a single unified pipeline.

1. Fluid Aerodynamics

Our external aerodynamic optimizer adjusts the shape scaling factors along the blade span. A thicker shape increases lift capacity but escalates profile drag. A thinner profile reduces aerodynamic drag but weakens the structure.

L_i = 0.5 * ρ_gas * W_i² * c_i * C_L(w_i) D_i = 0.5 * ρ_gas * W_i² * c_i * C_D(w_i)

The goal is to maximize the aerodynamic lift-to-drag ratio (L/D) while ensuring sufficient air deflection to extract rotational torque.

2. Conjugate Heat Transfer

The blade surface temperature is established by CHT energy balance. High-velocity gas heat flux enters the blade shell. Heat is extracted by internal cooling air flowing through the internal lattice channels.

T_surface = (h_gas * T_gas + h_cool * T_cool) / (h_gas + h_cool) h_cool = 15000.0 * sqrt(d_i) * (1 / w_i)^0.3

Increasing internal lattice density (d_i) enhances cooling capacity but adds solid mass. Decreasing external shape scale (w_i) reduces the heat transfer boundary area.

3. Structural Mechanics & Coupled Stress Analysis

Centrifugal stress and thermal expansion stress combine to form the total Von Mises stress. Crucially, the yield strength of Inconel 718 drops precipitously at high temperatures. The structural solver maps local CHT-derived blade temperatures to local yield capacities to check safety factors.

σ_combined = σ_centrifugal + σ_thermal σ_centrifugal = Σ [m_j * ω² * r_j] / A_effective_i SF_i = σ_yield(T_i) / σ_combined_i

A lower lattice density reduces the centrifugal force pulling from the root neck but increases the stress load area. The optimizer must dynamically balance material placement.

The Multi-Agent Swarm Workflow

AA

Aerodynamic Architect

Translates instructions into gas boundary conditions, CHT targets, and objective weight configurations.

CO

CFD Orchestrator

Parameterizes design profiles and executes gradient descent updates on shape scales and internal density fields.

FB

FSI Bridge

Coordinates partitioned coupling, mapping CFD surface temperatures and pressure vectors to FEA loads.

TC

Thermal Critic

Monitors limits. Automatically penalizes violations (temperature creep or aerodynamic slip) and triggers retraining.
