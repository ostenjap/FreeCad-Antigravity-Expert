● Structural Analysis — Root Cross-Section (8 000 RPM)

Three independent load cases are assessed at the blade root — the highest-stress location — and superimposed using the von Mises criterion. All loads are evaluated at the maximum continuous operating speed of 8 000 RPM, which produces a tip speed of 106.4 m/s (Ma 0.31).

◈ Leading-Edge Tubercles — Design & Benefits

What are tubercles?

Humpback whales have sinusoidal bumps along the leading edges of their pectoral fins. These tubercles modify the span-wise vorticity distribution, pinning small, stable vortices between bumps. The result is:

  • Delayed global stall onset (+2.9° effective AoA range)
  • Reduced drag through vortex mixing
  • Reduced tonal noise from smoother span-wise loading

LE_offset(r) = A * sin( 2π * r / λ )

A = 3 mm (amplitude)
λ = 40 mm (wavelength)
A/λ = 0.075 (amplitude ratio)
